# Rocky — character continuity sheet

## Character identity

Rocky is an anthropomorphic male cat CEO, approximately 30 years old, with golden-brown fur, sharp green eyes, and neatly styled dark-brown hair.

## Signature outfit

- Expensive black business suit
- White shirt
- Black tie
- Luxury wristwatch
- Polished black shoes

## Personality and expression

Confident, composed, and sometimes arrogant; wealthy executive presence.

## Visual treatment

Highly detailed fur and polished cinematic 3D animation. For a character reference image, use a full-body front view against a neutral studio background in a vertical 9:16 composition.

## Continuity lock

Keep Rocky's fur color, eye color, hairstyle, facial proportions, outfit, watch, and shoes consistent across every scene. Do not change clothing or add accessories unless the story explicitly calls for it. If Rocky enters or exits a scene, show his origin, route, direction, and final position rather than making him appear suddenly.

## Prompt reminder

Name every character present in a shot and specify each one's screen position and action. Do not omit a story-present character or add an unplanned character.
