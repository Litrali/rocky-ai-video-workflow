# Example: Rocky's first cinematic introduction

This is a planning example only. It does not generate images or video.

## Video overview

- Format: vertical short, target 9:16 (confirm model support)
- Style: cinematic 3D animation
- Main character: Rocky
- Sequence: establish the luxury office, reveal Rocky, finish on a confident hero frame.

## Shot 1 — Establishing office

**Timing:** 0–4 seconds  
**Framing:** Wide shot, slightly low camera angle.  
**Camera:** Slow, controlled push-in toward a luxury executive office.  
**Action:** Morning light enters through tall windows; the office remains otherwise calm.  
**Characters:** None. Do not add Rocky to this establishing shot.

## Shot 2 — Rocky enters

**Timing:** 4–8 seconds  
**Framing:** Medium-wide shot facing the office doorway.  
**Camera:** Gentle tracking movement that follows Rocky into the room.  
**Action:** Rocky enters from the doorway, walks toward the desk, and comes to a stop beside it. Show the complete route and direction of travel.  
**Characters:** Rocky only. Preserve his golden-brown fur, green eyes, dark-brown styled hair, black suit, white shirt, black tie, luxury watch, and polished black shoes.

## Shot 3 — Hero frame

**Timing:** 8–12 seconds  
**Framing:** Full-body front view, then a subtle push-in.  
**Camera:** Smooth push-in; keep Rocky centered and fully visible until the final framing change.  
**Action:** Rocky turns toward camera and holds a composed, confident expression.  
**Characters:** Rocky only.

## Continuity lock

Rocky's appearance and outfit remain unchanged. The office layout and lighting remain consistent between shots. Every entrance and movement has a visible origin and destination.
